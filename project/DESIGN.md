## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a modern, clinical-yet-warm wellness direct-to-consumer philosophy. Built for health-conscious consumers who prioritize transparent macros, functional performance, and clean living, the visual style combines the scientific rigor of high-performance nutrition with the tactile appeal of premium lifestyle branding.

The aesthetic philosophy draws on contemporary editorial minimalism balanced with dynamic performance typography. Crisp, airy neutral backgrounds establish high legibility and trust, while deliberate dark-mode contrast sections inject muscular depth and product gravitas. Structural components celebrate architectural precision: crisp stat-callout data boxes, pill-shaped utility badges, sharp typographic scale, and confident vertical rhythm inspired by premier global wellness brands.

## Colors

The color architecture creates a rhythmic visual cadence across long-form e-commerce landing pages. 

- **Primary Blue (`#1A63A5`) & Secondary Light Blue (`#4AA4D9`)**: Anchor transactional UI elements, primary buttons, actionable links, and interactive states. Light blue functions as an energetic highlight and hover surface.
- **Brand Orange (`#F58220`)**: Reserved strictly for high-visibility visual catalysts: promotion flashes, product announcement indicators, active badges, and conversion hooks.
- **Vegan Green (`#008A44`)**: Deployed exclusively for organic integrity, health affirmations, certified badges, and verified dietary trust factors.
- **Tonal Contrast Shift**: The default light environment (`#F8F9FA` canvas with `#FFFFFF` elevation tiles and `#102E5C` deep navy headlines) shifts decisively into immersive charcoal sections (`#121212` canvas with `#1E2022` card containers and `#374151` borders) for high-impact chapters such as the Benefits deep-dive.
- **Product Tones**: Vanilla Cream (`#E6D3C0`) and Fudge Brown (`#4A3B32`) operate as delicate background washes or warm micro-accents for ingredient storytelling and nutritional context.

## Typography

The typographic system pairs the energetic, geometric authority of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines, display numbers, and interface labels with the structured clarity of **Inter** for sustained technical and narrative copy.

- **Headings & Display**: Set in Plus Jakarta Sans with tightened negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) to deliver modern editorial weight. Headings above 32px automatically scale down on mobile breakpoints to preserve balance and prevent undesirable line breaks.
- **Stat Callouts**: Numbers utilize heavy weights (`800`) paired with tracked uppercase micro-labels (`letterSpacing: 0.06em`, `fontSize: 11px`), ensuring instant macro-nutrient legibility.
- **Eyebrows & Tickers**: Always uppercase with open tracking (`0.08em`) to demarcate category tags, certifications, and product sub-headers cleanly.
- **Body Text**: Inter handles all descriptive text, nutritional specifications, and ingredient definitions with optical line-heights between 150% and 160% to ensure fatigue-free scanning.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model utilizes a standard 12-column fluid grid system on desktop, collapsing to an 8-column layout on tablet, and a single- or 2-column flow on mobile. Maximum container width is pinned to `1280px` to maintain focused visual scanning.

- **Rhythm & Cadence**: Structural sections are separated by expansive vertical spacing (`space-2xl` on mobile, scaling to `space-3xl` on desktop) to emulate luxury editorial white space.
- **Stat & Macro Grids**: Metric cards (e.g., 18.8g protein, 4.8g fibre) lock into rigid 4-column row strips on desktop, shifting cleanly to 2×2 uniform blocks on tablet and mobile with tightly controlled `space-sm` or `space-md` gaps.
- **Responsive Adaptations**: Hero split-screens align copy and callouts to the left with dynamic product renders on the right, converting to stacked top-down vertical compositions on viewports beneath `768px`. Data-heavy nutrition tables enable seamless horizontal scroll containers with visual boundary fades when constrained by mobile screens.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy skeuomorphic drop shadows, relying instead on clean surface planar stratification, subtle ambient tinted shadows, and crisp ghost borders.

- **Light Mode Planar Hierarchy**: 
  - Ground tier: Base background canvas (`#F8F9FA`).
  - Elevated tier: Pure white surface tiles (`#FFFFFF`) framed by crisp, low-contrast 1px perimeter outlines (`#E5E7EB`).
  - Ambient Float: Primary interactive cards (e.g., product selection cards, hoverable trust cards) utilize an extra-diffused, cool-tinted shadow on hover: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(16, 46, 92, 0.08)`.
- **Dark Mode Elevation (Benefits & Performance Canvas)**:
  - Ground tier: Pitch black (`#121212`).
  - Elevated tier: Deep graphite containers (`#1E2022`) defined by structural slate seams (`border: 1px solid #374151`).
  - Depth is established strictly via luminous contrast: text renders in pure white or off-white (`#E2E8F0`), and accent icons carry muted glow halos or crisp monochrome borders rather than physical drop shadows.
- **Sticky UI & Sheets**: The header navigation and cart overlays deploy a blurred glass effect (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) at `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` with a faint baseline border (`#E5E7EB`).

## Shapes

The design system implements a deliberate duality: structural modern geometry for containers and cards, paired with smooth pill silhouettes for interactive chips, tags, and callouts.

- **Cards & Data Boxes**: Set at `rounded-md` (0.5rem / 8px) to `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px). Stat boxes and macro comparison tiles feature precise, engineered corners that convey scientific reliability.
- **Badges, Chips & Pills**: Set at full pill curves (`rounded-full` / 9999px). Used for ingredient tags (e.g., `No Added Sugar`), certification ribbons, nutrition chip highlights, and secondary micro-interactions.
- **Buttons**: Form factor is defined with a modern 8px radius (`0.5rem`) for primary conversion buttons to convey stability, while tertiary filter badges and quick-select tags retain full pill contours.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive CTAs
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#1A63A5` background, pure white bold text, 0.5rem border radius, padded with 14px vertical and 28px horizontal spacing. On hover, shifts to `#102E5C` with a smooth 150ms ease. Active state scales subtly down (`scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary Outlined**: Transparent background with a 1.5px border of `#1A63A5` (or `#E5E7EB` in neutral contexts), deep navy text. Hover fills the container with a soft tint (`rgba(26, 99, 165, 0.06)`).
- **Inverted / Dark Mode CTAs**: Solid white surface, `#121212` bold text, with crisp border transitions on hover.

### Badges & Status Chips
- **Certification Badges (Vegan Green)**: Solid `#008A44` background with white text, or soft green fill (`rgba(0, 138, 68, 0.1)`) with crisp `#008A44` text and an integrated checkmark or leaf glyph. Rendered as pill shapes with `label-sm` typography.
- **Alert / Promo Badges (Brand Orange)**: Background `#F58220`, white uppercase text, high-contrast, rounded pill.

### Stat Callout Boxes (Bulk-Style)
- Monolithic rectangular blocks with subtle 1px border (`#E5E7EB` on light, `#374151` on dark).
- Centered vertical layout: Heavy numeric macro value (`stat-number`) resting above a secondary tracked label (`stat-label`) in `#6B7280` or `#9CA3AF`. No drop shadows; completely flat and clinical.

### Cards & Comparison Modules
- **Standard Product & Info Cards**: Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid `#E5E7EB`, 16px corner radius, internal padding of 24px (`space-lg`). Smooth transform on hover (`translateY(-4px)`).
- **Dark Benefit Cards**: Background `#1E2022`, border `#374151`, icon placed inside a circular container with accent-tinted borders, white headings, and muted body text (`#9CA3AF`).
- **Problem vs. Solution Split Cards**: Problem column utilizes muted slate containers with subtle crimson or neutral badges; Solution column utilizes white surface, `#008A44` checks, and a subtle border highlight in `#4AA4D9`.

### Form Controls & Inputs
- **Text Inputs**: Clean 48px height, `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#E5E7EB` border, 8px radius, text in `#1F2937`. Focus transitions to a 1.5px `#1A63A5` ring with zero offset.
- **Selection Radios & Subscription Toggles**: Pill cards with embedded radio circles. When selected, border color activates to `#1A63A5` with a faint blue wash background (`rgba(26, 99, 165, 0.04)`).

### Nutrition Data Table
- Alternating subtle rows (`#FFFFFF` to `#F8F9FA`), clean 1px horizontal dividers (`#E5E7EB`).
- Bold font weights for primary macros (Energy, Protein, Dietary Fibre) with right-aligned values and percentage RDA metrics.
